# Listener profile

The podcast has exactly one listener: Lucas. He listens on a ~30 minute bike ride to work.

## Who he is
- Economic researcher by profession, based in the Netherlands.
- Hands-on with AI: builds Claude skills, RAG applications and other AI tooling himself.
- Interested in solving real problems with AI through automation, not necessarily by building SaaS.

## What he wants to hear about (roughly in order of priority)
1. **Autonomous AI that is safe and productive**: agent architectures, permissions, guardrails, hooks,
   sandboxing, prompt injection defence, evals, human-in-the-loop design. How to configure AI so it can
   work on its own while his own role stays small. This is what he thinks about most right now.
2. **AI workflows, architectures and techniques**: RAG (retrieval, chunking, reranking, hybrid search,
   GraphRAG), agent design patterns, skills, tool use, MCP, memory, evaluation, cost/latency trade-offs.
3. **New models and developments**, both closed and open source/open weights: what is new, what it is
   actually good at, what it costs, whether it changes what you'd build.
4. **AI for (economic) research**: literature review, data work, replication, forecasting, text-as-data,
   agent-assisted research, and the economics of AI itself (productivity, labour, investment, compute).
5. **Business ideas**: concrete automation opportunities he could work on, preferably in a Dutch context
   (Dutch regulation, sectors, government, SMEs, EU AI Act implications, subsidies like WBSO).

## What he does not want
- Hype without substance, funding rounds without a "so what", consumer gadget news, celebrity drama.
- Generic beginner explanations of what an LLM is.
- The same story two days in a row unless there is real news on it.

## Level
Technically literate, likes depth on architecture and trade-offs, but also enjoys broader ideas.
Explain mechanisms, not buzzwords.
